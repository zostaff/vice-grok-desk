# README artwork

Art direction by zostaff. References: the existing pumpcraft, mev-streamer, and zostaff profile READMEs. Palette: midnight aubergine, hot pink, mint cyan; terminal textures and Vice City imagery.

`hero.webp` was generated with the built-in image generation tool, then encoded as WebP for GitHub. Brand imagery is an illustrative interpretation, not an official logo asset or endorsement. Vector diagrams, section banners, and footer are regenerated with `python3 scripts/readme-art.py`. The terminal illustration reflects the bundled synthetic fixture, not live trading.

Art direction: dark near-black midnight aubergine backdrop, Vice City Miami coastal skyline at night, palm silhouettes, peach sunset thin horizon, neon hot pink and mint cyan, subtle terminal ASCII texture and fine scanlines. Refined editorial tech poster, high contrast, generous spacing, no clutter, no floating UI cards. Compose title on left 60 percent with graphic scene on right. Right side: prominently show recognizable white Grok circular slash logo alongside authentic-style Grand Theft Auto VI logo, stacked white outlined grand theft auto lettering in front of large pink-purple VI Roman numeral, palms inside VI. These are reference brands for a fan research project, not official collaboration. Put small readable "UNOFFICIAL / RESEARCH" in bottom right. No profit figures, no live trading claims, no fake screenshots. Extremely crisp professional artwork, beautiful luminous edges but restrained glow, subtle pink/cyan ASCII motif behind scene. All text exactly spelled, creator attribution only zostaff. Save the generated image to disk and provide its file path.
